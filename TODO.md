# Roadmap

Each release adds one large function. A task leaves this list when its release
is out. The reason for each choice is in [docs/decisions.md](docs/decisions.md).

## 0.4.0: Grades

- The credit-weighted average on the scale from 4 to 10.
- A what-if mode: the score that you need on the final for a grade.

## 0.5.0: Exams

- The dates of the exam session, with a countdown for each course.
- Reminders one week, three days, and one day before.

## 0.6.0: Deadlines

- Lab reports and assignments, with local notifications.

## 1.0.0

- All the functions above, checked together on a real iPhone.

## After 1.0.0

- An attendance tracker for each course.
- Quick links to the notes, the PDFs, and the lab sheets of each course.
- A study planner that spreads the revision over the days before each exam.
